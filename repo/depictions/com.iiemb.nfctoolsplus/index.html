NFCTools Plus

Control Center quick-emulate tiles for NFCTools

v0.7.13~beta+100RootlessRoothide

NFCTools Plus is the jailbreak companion for the NFCTools app. It brings quick-access virtual tag emulation straight into iOS system surfaces, for rootless and RootHide jailbreaks.

Tag data comes from the NFCTools app — assign tiles to saved tags in NFCTools › Settings. Ships as a tweak only; the NFCTools app itself is installed separately as an .ipa through TrollStore.

Control Center quick emulation

  • Four Control Center tiles ("NFC Tag 1–4") start emulating their assigned tag from anywhere, even with the screen locked.
  • A floating on-screen HUD shows the active tag name, UID, a radiating transmission wave, and a quick Stop action.
  • Emulation always stops on its own after 5–30 seconds (default 10).

Command-line tool (nfctools)

A privileged CLI over Terminal or SSH:

  • nfctools emulate <tile 1-4 | uuid | uid | name> — start emulating a tag
  • nfctools stop — stop the active emulation
  • nfctools list [-v] — list saved tags and tile assignments
  • nfctools assign <tile 1-4> <name|uid|uuid|none> — assign a tag to a tile
  • nfctools autostop [5-30] — get or set the auto-stop countdown
  • nfctools status — show the current emulation state

Requirements

  • A rootless (Dopamine, palera1n) or RootHide jailbreak.
  • The NFCTools app installed separately (via TrollStore).
  • An iPhone whose NFC controller supports direct access (e.g. iPhone 12 / 13 and newer). The app's Diagnostics page tells you whether your device can use each feature.

0.7.2

Added

Changed

  • New Home: your saved tags are now a stack of cards in each tag's color and icon. Swipe left or right to move through them (it loops), and tap ▶ on a card to emulate it.

  • Settings now opens as a sheet.
  • New Read screen: waves at the top of the screen show where the iPhone's NFC reader is, so you know where to hold the tag.
  • New Emulate screen: shows the tag's card, and flashes and vibrates when a reader touches your iPhone.
  • Write, Update from Tag and Clone now use the same screen as Read, each in its own color, showing the saved tag's card.

Fixed

  • Reading a tag no longer closes the Read screen straight away when the NFC service is busy and NFCTools restarts it.
  • If the NFC service doesn't answer, the Read screen now closes with a message after a few seconds instead of staying on "Starting reader…".
