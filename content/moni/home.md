**Manage Your Finances with Ease Using Our Money Manager App!**

Take control of your financial life with our comprehensive Money Manager app. Designed to simplify your financial management.

Whether you're budgeting for the month, saving for a big purchase, or simply trying to manage your daily expenses, **Moni** is the perfect tool to help you achieve your financial goals. Start managing your money smarter today!

### Features

-   👛 **Wallets** for cash, cards and e-wallets, each in its own currency, converted to your main currency with live exchange rates

-   🧾 **Expenses, income and transfers** with categories, tags, notes and photos

-   🎯 **Budgets** for the categories and wallets you choose, daily, weekly, monthly or annually

-   🔁 **Schedules** that add recurring transactions such as bills and salary automatically

-   📊 **Reports** for cash flow, net worth, and spending by category and tag

-   ☁️ **Google Drive backup** with encrypted backups and optional Auto Backup

-   🔒 **App lock** with a PIN, fingerprint or face unlock

-   📤 **Export and import** transactions as CSV or Excel files
