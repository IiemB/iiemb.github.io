# Getting Started

#### Q: What is Moni?

**A:** Moni is a personal finance app for tracking your expenses, income and budgets. You record transactions in your wallets, set budgets for the categories you care about, and see where your money goes with charts and summaries.

#### Q: Do I need an account to use Moni?

**A:** No. You can choose **Continue Without Sign In** and use every feature with your data stored only on your phone. Signing in with Google is optional and is only used for backups to your Google Drive.

#### Q: Which platforms is Moni available on?

**A:** Moni is available on Android through [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.iiemb.moni).

#### Q: Which languages does Moni support?

**A:** English and Bahasa Indonesia. You can change the language in Settings; Moni asks you to restart the app to apply it. If you would like to help translate Moni into another language, contact <bayyinullah@gmail.com>.

#### Q: Can I try Moni without entering my own data?

**A:** Yes. **Demo Mode** fills the app with generated sample data so you can explore it. The demo data is deleted when you leave Demo Mode, and some features (such as backups) are not available while it is on.

# Wallets & Transactions

#### Q: What is a wallet?

**A:** A wallet is anywhere you keep money, such as cash, a bank card or an e-wallet. Each wallet has its own name, starting balance, icon, colour and currency. New users start with **Cash** and **Card** wallets, and you can add, reorder or hide wallets at any time.

#### Q: What types of transactions can I record?

**A:** Three types:

- **Expense**: money going out.
- **Income**: money coming in.
- **Transfer**: moving money between your own wallets. Transfers don't count as income or spending.

Each transaction can include a category, tags, a note and a photo from your camera or gallery.

#### Q: What are unpaid transactions?

**A:** You can mark a transaction as **unpaid**, for example a bill that is due later. Unpaid transactions don't count toward your totals until they are paid. You can set a reminder for the due date, or let Moni mark the transaction as paid automatically when it is due.

#### Q: What happens when I delete a wallet or a category?

**A:** Deleting a wallet also deletes all of its transactions. Deleting a category keeps its transactions and marks them as **Uncategorized**. If you want to combine two categories instead, use **Merge**.

#### Q: How do I fix a wallet balance that doesn't match my real balance?

**A:** Use **balance correction** on the wallet. It records the difference so the balance in Moni matches your actual balance.

#### Q: Can I use more than one currency?

**A:** Yes. Each wallet can use its own currency, and Moni converts amounts to your **main currency** for totals and charts. Exchange rates are updated online and saved, so the last known rates keep working offline. You can also set decimal places, a short number format and where the currency symbol appears.

#### Q: Are there shortcuts for common actions?

**A:** Yes. Swipe a transaction to the right in the list to bookmark or update it, and use **Duplicate** to repeat a past transaction quickly. The amount field has a built-in calculator, and on Android you can long-press the Moni icon for an **Add Transaction** shortcut.

# Budgets & Schedules

#### Q: How do budgets work?

**A:** A budget sets a spending limit for the categories and wallets you choose, over a **daily, weekly, monthly or annual** period. Moni shows how much of the limit you have used in the current period, and you can look back at previous periods.

#### Q: What are schedules?

**A:** Schedules create recurring transactions automatically, such as a salary or a subscription. They can repeat daily, weekly, on weekdays, on weekends, monthly, at the start or end of the month, or annually. You can set an end date, skip a single run or add the next one now, and Moni notifies you when a scheduled transaction is added.

#### Q: Can Moni remind me to record my transactions?

**A:** Yes. Turn on the daily **Transaction Reminder** and pick a time. Moni also sends reminders for unpaid transactions on their due dates. Allow notifications for Moni so reminders can appear.

# Reports & Widgets

#### Q: What reports does Moni have?

**A:** The overview shows your cash flow, balance chart, net worth, income and expenses, and summaries by category and tag, for all time or for a day, week, month or year. You can choose which items appear on the home screen.

#### Q: How do I find a transaction?

**A:** Use **Search**. It matches transaction names, wallets, categories, tags, amounts and notes.

#### Q: Is there a home-screen widget?

**A:** Yes. The **Net Worth** widget shows your total balance on your home screen. While the app lock is on, the widget hides the amounts.

# Backup & Data

#### Q: Where is my data stored?

**A:** On your phone, in Moni's private storage. Your financial data is never sent to the developer's servers. If you sign in with Google, you can also keep backups in your own Google Drive.

#### Q: How do Google Drive backups work?

**A:** Sign in with Google, then press **Backup**. The backup is saved in a private app folder in your Google Drive, so it doesn't appear among your normal Drive files and only Moni can read it. A backup contains your wallets, transactions, schedules, categories, budgets and tags.

You can also turn on **Auto Backup**, which creates or replaces a single "AutoBackup" file whenever you open the app.

#### Q: Are my photos included in backups?

**A:** No. Transaction photos are not backed up.

#### Q: How do I move my data to a new phone?

**A:** On your old phone, create a backup. On the new phone, install Moni and sign in with the same Google account during setup. Moni will find your backups and ask you to choose one to restore.

#### Q: Will restoring a backup replace my current data?

**A:** No. Restoring **combines** the backup with the data already on your phone, including transactions, wallets, tags, categories and budgets. To get exactly the backed-up data, restore it on a fresh install or after using **Delete All**.

#### Q: Can I keep a backup file myself?

**A:** Yes. You can export any backup as a **.monibak** file and share or save it wherever you like. Backup files are encrypted. To use one, choose **Import** and select the .monibak file (you need to be signed in with Google).

#### Q: What happens if I uninstall Moni without a backup?

**A:** Your data is stored only on your phone, so uninstalling the app deletes it. Create a backup first if you want to keep your data.

#### Q: Can I export my transactions to a spreadsheet?

**A:** Yes. The **Exporter** saves your transactions as **CSV** or **Excel (.xlsx)** with the date, amount, category, wallet, tags and note. You can choose the period and the wallets to export.

#### Q: Can I import transactions from another app?

**A:** Yes, from a CSV file with these columns: Date (for example `18/Nov/2026 09:30`), Amount, Type, Category, Wallet, Tags and Note. Wallets and categories that don't exist yet are created for you. Download the example file in the import screen to see the exact format.

# Security & Privacy

#### Q: Can I lock Moni?

**A:** Yes. Set a **PIN** of 3 to 6 digits and, if your phone supports it, unlock with your **fingerprint or face**. You can choose how long Moni waits before locking again after you leave the app.

#### Q: What if I forget my PIN?

**A:** Moni has no PIN recovery. If you can't unlock the app, you will need to reinstall it, which deletes the data on your phone, and then restore your latest backup. Keeping regular backups protects you from this.

#### Q: Does Moni collect any data?

**A:** Moni doesn't collect your financial data. It collects anonymous usage and crash information to help us fix bugs and improve the app. See the [Terms & Privacy Policy](/moni/policies) for details.

#### Q: Does Moni show ads?

**A:** No. Moni has no ads.

# Moni Pro

#### Q: Is Moni free?

**A:** Yes. Moni is free to use. **Moni Pro** is an optional subscription that removes the limits of the free plan and supports the developer.

#### Q: What does Moni Pro include?

**A:** Moni Pro gives you:

- Unlimited budgets (the free plan includes 2).
- Unlimited schedules (the free plan includes 2).
- Excel export in addition to CSV.
- Export for any period (the free plan exports today or the current week).
- Every colour scheme (the free plan includes 3).

#### Q: How much does Moni Pro cost?

**A:** Moni Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription in Google Play. The current price for your country is shown in the app.

#### Q: Can I try Pro features for free?

**A:** Yes. Choose **Unlock For Free** to use Pro features for 24 hours. You can renew it whenever it ends. Unlimited schedules are only included in the paid subscription.

#### Q: How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

**A:** Subscriptions renew automatically and are managed by Google Play, so you can cancel at any time in Google Play. If you reinstall Moni or change phones, tap **Restore Purchases** to get your subscription back.

# Help

#### Q: Moni says "Please check your internet connection" when I back up.

**A:** Backups and restores need an internet connection. Check your connection and try again. If you closed the Google sign-in window, Moni shows "Operation cancelled"; sign in again to continue.

#### Q: Import failed. What can I do?

**A:** Moni can import **.monibak** backup files and **.csv** files. Make sure the file has the right extension, and for CSV files that the columns and date format match the example file.

#### Q: A setting didn't change anything.

**A:** Some settings, such as the language, only apply after a restart. Use **Restart App** in the menu, or close and reopen Moni.

**A:** Email <bayyinullah@gmail.com>, or send feedback from the app's menu.
